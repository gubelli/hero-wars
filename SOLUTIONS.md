## Why the perfect match wins

The game shows these after the last fight via **Show best solution**.

- **Captain Unlabelled → Mr. Power BI** (DAX sorcery 98): a dashboard can’t be built on col_1, col_2 and col_3, so naming every field in a clean data model is the first thing he does.
- **The API Breaker → Mr. DevOps** (Pipeline power 99): every change goes through one pipeline, where contract tests stop a renamed field before it ships.
- **The Quick-Favour Phantom → Mr. MESI** (Questions deflected 96): deflecting questions is his daily job, so “just one tiny thing” bounces straight off.
- **The Flaky Fiend → Mr. Gleitschirm** (Curiosity 100): flaky tests survive because everyone hits “re-run”; his endless “how does that work?” digs out the root cause.
- **The Refresh Wraith → Mr. Handoff Plugin** (Handoff speed 97): a failed refresh is a broken connection between two systems – moving data between apps is his superpower.
- **The Hard-Code Hydra → Mr. Salesforcechat** (Do-it-yourself power 99): waiting for someone else only grows new heads; he rewrites it himself.
- **The Gate-Change Gremlin → Mr. Sekretariat** (Process mastery 99): he turns chaos into a process – new flight, new plan, new pickup, signed off.
- **The Blank Ticket → Mr. Research** (Data digging 98): finding out what the reporter actually meant is a research problem.
- **The Waiting Warden → Mrs. Coordinator** (Ticket triage 97): tickets only sleep in “Waiting” when nobody chases them; she keeps every ticket on track.
- **The Grey Sky → Mr. Flughafentaxi** (Early-morning pickups 98): nobody can beat the weather, but he can get Mr. Gleitschirm to the airport at 4 a.m. and off to sunny skies.
